Kutná Hora, a small town in Bohemia built on silver. The grey Gothic cathedral with its cluster of conical spires dominates the hill, with a long baroque college and a terrace of stone statues leading toward it, while vineyards climb the slopes below and red roofs fill the valley.

The streets are cobbled and quiet, with pastel townhouses, a plague column on the square, painted gables and the odd antique shop. On the edge of town is the ossuary chapel, where the walls, arches and a great chandelier are made entirely of human bones. It was a grey, overcast day, which suited the place.
