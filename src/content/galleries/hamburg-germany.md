Hamburg is brick and water. I spent most of the time around the Speicherstadt, where the old warehouses with their green copper turrets line the canals and the light at dusk doubles every facade in the still water. The Rathaus towers over the centre, the elevated trains curve past glass offices, and the ruined walls of St. Nikolai stand open to the sky between modern towers.

Down at the harbour there were sailing ships and a paddle steamer, blue cranes, a floating dry dock and a man painting the deck of an orange tugboat. In between, the small things: shoes hanging from a birch, a model ship in a barred window, a fairground Ferris wheel above a meadow of orange lilies.
