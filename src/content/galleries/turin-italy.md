Turin is where I live, so these are not holiday photos but years of walking the same streets with a camera. The Mole Antonelliana turns up from every angle: over the brick rooftops, between a dome and a bell tower, reflected in a glass office facade, with the Alps behind it from the hill across the Po. The rest is mostly at night: trams and light trails under the arcades, Piazza Castello with its red-lit fountains, the Porta Palatina, the cathedral and the onion domes of the synagogue, fireworks over the river.

There are quieter days too: a tram crossing a stone bridge above the rushing Po, a domed church in falling snow, street performers asleep in a bed on a square.

*I took these pictures in 2007-2013.*
