Salou, a resort town on the Costa Dorada. The days are spent on a crowded beach and out on the water, with parasails above the bay and pedal boats drifting in front of the apartment blocks. From the rocks and from the balconies the coast opens up, and the sunsets turn the mountains behind the bay dark orange.

In between there is the other Salou: wide pedestrian promenades lined with palms, a fountain at the end of a paved plaza, umbrella pines in front of the hotels, and the beach map at the top of the sand where everyone starts the day.
