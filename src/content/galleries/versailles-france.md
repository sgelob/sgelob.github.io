Versailles, on a bright summer day. The golden gates and crowned crests of the palace glitter above the crowds queuing at the entrance, and in the forecourt a bronze horseman stands between huge arcs of rusted steel, modern sculpture among the old stone.

Behind the palace the gardens unfold in perfect order: the Latona fountain with its gilded frogs, the long avenue down to the Grand Canal, the Orangery parterre with potted trees around a round pool, and rows of clipped conical hedges on the lawns.
