A trip between Lake Como and Lake Lugano. The paths run through hazel woods and dark spruce forest, past mossy walls, a pair of donkeys looking over a fence, and a fire salamander on the wet ground, with small villages and the lake glimpsed far below.

In Como itself the weather was grey and hazy: walkers on the pier, ferries by the striped mooring poles, a small boat crossing the water, and the marble cathedral lit up at night. A funicular climbs the hill above the town, and from the mountain terrace the view opens over autumn leaves, the lake and the snowy Alps in the distance.
