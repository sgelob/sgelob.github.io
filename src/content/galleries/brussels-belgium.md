Brussels under low grey skies and the odd umbrella. The Grand Place is the centre of it all: the Gothic town hall tower, the gilded guild houses, the cobbles full of people looking up. From there it is a short walk to the tiny Manneken Pis in one of his costumes, a bronze dog on the pavement, narrow lanes of restaurant awnings and a glass-roofed arcade.

Further out the city changes scale: a park with a triumphal arch, the green-domed basilica on its hill, the glass towers of the European quarter, and the Atomium, whose mirrored spheres look best from underneath.
