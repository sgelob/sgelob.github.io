Monte Carlo is a small place where everything is stacked up the hillside: apartment towers, cream-coloured hotels and stone stairways, with the mountains right behind and the sea in front. The Casino is the centrepiece, and its ornate towers and green domes look best against a deep blue sky, with a mirrored sphere shining in the gardens just below.

Away from the grand facades there are quieter corners: palms over the water, a garden of cacti and succulents, a sculpture of a bird with open wings among the trees. The harbour is full of white yachts, and at dusk the whole coast lights up from the hills above.
