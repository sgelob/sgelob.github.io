Lloret de Mar, on the Costa Brava. The town beach is a long strip of sand packed with umbrellas and sunbathers, with palm trees and apartment blocks behind it, and a street running straight down to the blue water.

The best part is just beyond the sand. A footpath follows the coast along a low stone wall, through a short tunnel cut into the rock, past an anchor and a bronze plaque set into the cliff, and under the pines. From up there the sea is clear and very blue, the rocks are pale grey, a sailboat leaves a long white wake, and a crenellated castle stands on a wooded headland above the water.
