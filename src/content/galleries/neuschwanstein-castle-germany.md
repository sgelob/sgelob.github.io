Neuschwanstein in the Bavarian Alps, seen in cold winter light. The white castle sits on its rocky spur above the forest, with pointed towers that I photographed from the valley, from the path below the walls, and from a viewpoint over the gorge, with lakes and plains stretching behind it.

Across the valley stands the yellow castle of Hohenschwangau, appearing between bare trees. Around the two there is the rest of the setting: a painted chalet, a small white church alone in the meadows, and the still, dark Alpsee with snow-capped peaks in its reflection and a few ducks on the water.
