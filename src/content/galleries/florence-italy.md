Florence in warm light. The terracotta dome of the cathedral keeps appearing at the end of narrow streets, between ochre houses and laundry balconies, and from close up it turns into green, white and pink marble, with the gilded bronze doors of the baptistery next to it. I wandered with no plan through the small lanes: a wine shop under a stone arch, a yellow corner café with a bicycle, graffiti on old green doors.

The Ponte Vecchio and its leaning houses look best from the river banks at sunset, with rowing boats on the water. Between the Uffizi courtyard and the Piazza della Signoria the crowds move under the arches, and the white Neptune stands against the brick towers.
