A day of sun and sea at Alassio, on the Ligurian coast. The beach is a long curve of grey sand, lined with striped umbrellas and deckchairs, and the waves keep rolling in under a pale summer sky. Behind the sand stand pastel houses with palm trees in front, and green hills climb straight up behind them.

The pier is the place for a slow walk, with its lamp posts, benches and everyone out for an evening stroll. From the water you can see a small stone chapel on a rock, a wooded headland and, further out, the dark shape of a small island on the horizon. Calm, hot, and very Italian.
