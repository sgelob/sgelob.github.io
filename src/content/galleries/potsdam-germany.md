A foggy winter day in the parks of Potsdam, where everything is soft and muted. The yellow facade of Sanssouci and its green lattice pavilion, the gilded Chinese House among bare branches, the long pink-brick front of the New Palace across a wet courtyard, the campanile of the Friedenskirche next to still water.

In between there are gravel paths, tunnels of trained hedges, and a frozen canal with one patch of open water and a duck. Hardly anyone about, and the fog took the horizon away.
