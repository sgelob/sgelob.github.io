Paris, in sun and in winter grey. The big names are all here: the Eiffel Tower from its feet, from inside its iron lattice and from the top, Notre-Dame, the Arc de Triomphe, the Louvre pyramid, Sacré-Cœur above its lawn full of sunbathers. But I kept stopping for the small things too: Art Nouveau Métro entrances, a Space Invader mosaic on a stone corner, a man playing an old piano on the pavement, a painter on a folding stool by the river.

After dark the city changes: the Louvre courtyard almost empty, Notre-Dame floodlit across its square, the Moulin Rouge in red neon, and the Seine running gold under the bridges.
