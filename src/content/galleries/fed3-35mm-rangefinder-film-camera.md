- **Name:** “ФЭД-3”
- **Producer:** FED
- **Year:** 1968
- **Serial Number:** 8494016
- **Frame size:** 24x36 mm
- **Lens:** Industar-61 2.8/52 (И-61, 2181468)
- **Shutter:** 1s, 1/2s, 1/4s, 1/8s, 1/15s, 1/30s, 1/60s, 1/125s, 1/250s, 1/500s + B.

The FED 3 was made in Kharkiv from 1961 to 1979. Compared with the [FED 2](/gallery/fed2-35mm-rangefinder-film-camera/) it adds slow shutter speeds down to one second, and the mechanism that slows the shutter took enough room in the body that the rangefinder base was shortened to 41 mm. This one is the later version, with a lever to wind the film instead of a knob. On these the first digit of the seven-digit serial number gives the year, so 8494016 points to 1968.

On top, the frame counter beside the release button carries a small exposure calculator: film speeds in ГОСТ, the Soviet standard, and symbols for the weather. It came with its brown leather case and strap, worn at the edges, with FED 3 pressed into the lens cap.
