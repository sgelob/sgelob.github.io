Pisa, and not only the tower. On the big lawn the white marble of the cathedral, the baptistery and the leaning tower glows under a grey summer sky, with crowds posing in front of it, as everyone does.

But the city is more than that. I wandered along the Arno, where ochre and orange palaces line the embankment and the bridges and a small spired Gothic church look at themselves in the water. In the old streets I found carved coats of arms, faded frescoes under the arcades, a red flag with a white cross hanging next to the European one, a street vendor leaning on a wall, and a red neon sign glowing in a narrow alley.
