A few days in Prague. The city shows itself from above first: red roofs, the green domes and Gothic spires of the castle hill, and the Žižkov TV tower with its crawling babies standing over everything. Down at street level I kept finding details: gilded signs, sculpted doorways, painted facades, a puppet theatre, an astronomical clock surrounded by tourists.

Old Town Square and the Týn Church are the busy heart of it, but the quieter corners were my favourites: cobbled lanes between yellow houses, the river with its swans and tour boats, the old bridges, and Wenceslas Square at night. Prague by day is orange and gold, and after dark it turns to floodlit stone.
