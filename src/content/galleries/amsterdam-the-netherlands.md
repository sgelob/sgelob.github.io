Amsterdam, seen mostly from the water. A small boat takes me along the canals under low grey clouds, past houseboats with picket fences, tall brick houses leaning slightly over the quays, and stone bridges lined up one behind another. From the harbour come the green hull of NEMO, the trains by Central Station and the big I amsterdam letters.

Then the Pride canal parade takes over: boats covered in pink balloons, rainbow flags, feather boas and confetti, and crowds packed on every bridge and leaning out of windows. In between come the quieter things: gabled facades, bicycles everywhere, blue trams, the lawns of Museumplein and the shade of Vondelpark.
