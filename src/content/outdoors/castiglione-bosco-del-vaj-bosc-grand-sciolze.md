Forty-four kilometres and 1,497 metres of climbing on the cross-country bike, east along the hills above the Po through the two woods of Castagneto and Casalborgone, the Bosco del Vaj and the Bosc Grand, and back by Rivalba and Sciolze.

The way out drops past Gassino and Bussolino and climbs from San Raffaele to Mezzavilla, high above Castagneto, where a giant spider clung to a garden wall by an iron gate.

The Bosco del Vaj has been a nature reserve since 1978, and with the Bosc Grand it is now a protected site of European interest. Its value lies in the long history of the wood itself and in the oddities the last ice ages left behind in its plants, and the rocks of these hills have given up fossils of plants and animals millions of years old since the eighteenth century.

The way back crosses Casalborgone and climbs over Rivalba, then runs through the woods of Mondesio below Sciolze and comes back on path 82 above Gassino.
