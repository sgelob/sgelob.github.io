Thirteen and a half kilometres and 382 metres of climbing on foot, a figure of eight over the hill between Gassino and Sciolze.

The first loop climbs onto the slopes above Gassino on rough tracks and sunken paths, through meadows of tall grass and spring woods, with the green hills rolling away under a line of clouds. A dirt path crosses a field past an old farm, and narrow paths run through the grass and into deep shade under the trees.

The second loop heads east towards Sciolze on grassy double tracks through flowering meadows and gravel lanes between the hedges, past a trail sign by a fallen log and under arching branches. Sciolze comes into view on its hill beyond the fields, and the last stretch comes back along the roads below it.
