Forty-eight kilometres and 1,360 metres of climbing on the cross-country bike, a long loop over the hill to San Mauro and Baldissero, east through Montaldo, Sciolze and Rivalba to Castagneto, and back along the Po.

Mist lay in the valleys below the wooded hills, and the first trails were narrow, squeezed between brambles, one of them blocked by a fallen tree that someone had already sawn into logs.

Over the ridge above Rivodora the roads were deep in dead leaves, and beyond Montaldo they ran along grassy banks towards Sciolze, its houses and church tower gathered on the hilltop ahead.

Past Rivalba and Castagneto the route drops to San Raffaele Cimena. The village takes its name from a chapel to the archangel on the old road from Turin to Chivasso, and in the Middle Ages, harried by raiders on the plain, most of it moved up into the hills. Its parish church was built in 1726 as a grand enlargement of a chapel of 1594.

Down on the plain by Gassino the farm tracks between the winter fields had turned to deep mud, puddles standing in every rut.
