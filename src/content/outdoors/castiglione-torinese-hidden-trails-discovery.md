Twenty-seven kilometres and 1,262 metres of climbing on the trail bike, on the hill between Castiglione, Gassino, Bardassano and Cordova, with a purpose: several of the trails here were not on any map, and I went out to find them and ride them, then added them to OpenStreetMap.

OpenStreetMap is the free map of the world built by volunteers, and it is the base of many of the maps used outdoors: Komoot draws its map, its routing and its points of interest from it. A path that is missing there is missing for anyone planning a route, so every one added makes the hill a little easier to discover.

The search went from the woods above Gassino and along the Strada della Trinità, east to Bardassano, round by Pavarolo and up to Cordova, then down into the Valle Garavaglia and up again. In March the woods were still mostly bare, the trails deep in last year’s leaves and striped with long shadows, the first green only just showing. Across the valley the castle of Bardassano stood on its hill above trees in white blossom, and the last stretch ran across a meadow back towards the village.
