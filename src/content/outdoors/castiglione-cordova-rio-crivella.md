Two walks to the waterfall of the Rio Crivella, two and a half years apart, put together here. The track is the longer one, from September 2023: nine kilometres and 254 metres of climbing on foot, over Cordova and down through the Valle Garavaglia.

In September the hillside meadows were mown, with hay bales lying in the grass and the plain spreading out in the haze below. The way climbs on lanes and gravel tracks to Cordova, then dives into the woods on sunken paths under arching branches and narrow trails through the undergrowth, past Tetti Francesi and down a stony path between tall grass and young trees into the valley.

The first walk, in April 2021, was a shorter one, five kilometres, straight up the valley and back. It was the better season for the waterfall: the wood was carpeted with the green leaves of wild garlic, the paths ran between them like narrow ribbons, and at the end of the gorge, between walls of pebbly rock, the stream was falling in a full white curtain into its pool.
