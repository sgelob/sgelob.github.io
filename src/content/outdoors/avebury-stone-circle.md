Just over two kilometres on foot, a short loop round the stone circle of Avebury in Wiltshire, under a grey March sky.

Avebury is a henge, a huge circular bank and ditch with a ring of standing stones inside it, and a village has grown up in the middle of it all. Wikipedia gives the outer circle a diameter of about 330 metres, the largest in Britain, and dates the start of its building to around 2850 BC.

The walk follows the chalk path along the top of the great bank, with the muddy ditch below, past old beeches whose roots spill over the bank, and down among the grey stones standing in the grass.
