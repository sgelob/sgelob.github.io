Fifteen kilometres and 313 metres of climbing on foot, a loop in the Monferrato hills round Cocconato, up to the hamlet of Maroero and back through the valley.

The way climbs from the valley on grassy tracks along the fields, past lone trees standing in the meadows, into a landscape of rolling hills and villages on their tops under a dappled sky. Lanes run under tall trees and through green tunnels, and purple bellflowers grew along the banks.

Past Cocconito the route follows the ridge to Maroero, a scattering of houses with a small nineteenth-century church dedicated to the Madonna delle Grazie, where the wooded valleys fade away into the haze on every side. Cocconato itself, on the next hill, shows its brick lanes and bell tower.

The way down by Bonvino and Bausignano drops into the valley, past a stream green with weed, poplars and a field of young wheat, and the last stretch follows a gravel track beside an old railway, its rails lost in the grass.
