Thirteen kilometres and 418 metres of climbing on foot, a walk over the hill between Castiglione and Gassino, up to Cordova and round by Bardassano.

A narrow road climbs through the woods and opens onto a meadow with the valley and the plain below, then climbs past an old off-roader so overgrown with squash leaves that it seemed to be turning back into the hillside.

From Cordova the path drops into the woods: a hazel coppice with the leaves already falling, slopes buried under brambles and ivy, and a trail between logs freshly sawn from a fallen tree. Towards Bardassano the woods give way to orchards and meadows, the green hills folding round a farmhouse under big clouds.

Above Bardassano a lane runs under tall trees, with a Palestinian flag hanging by a fence, before the way down towards Gassino.
