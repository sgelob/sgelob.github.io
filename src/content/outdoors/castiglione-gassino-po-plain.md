Thirteen kilometres and 147 metres of climbing on the cross-country bike, a short, almost flat loop on the plain between the hill and the Po.

Instead of climbing, the route stays low, on gravel roads between the fields and along the edge of the meadows, with the wooded hill on one side and the river somewhere beyond the trees on the other, under a sky piling up with big summer clouds.

By the road a row of poplars stands over a small hand-made sign, a rebus: an eye and the words “al tubo”, to be read as occhio al tubo, mind the pipe.
