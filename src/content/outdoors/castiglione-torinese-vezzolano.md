Forty-five kilometres and 1,200 metres of climbing on the cross-country bike, east across the hills to the abbey of Vezzolano below Albugnano, and back by Berzano, Cinzano and Sciolze.

It was a clear autumn day. The way out crosses the hill by Pavarolo and drops to Marentino, on grassy tracks along the hedges and across the meadows, with the red roofs of Avuglione below and the hills folding away towards the Monferrato.

Beyond Arignano and Moncucco a sunken path runs under the ivy, and then the abbey appears between two palm trees. Its front is brick banded with sandstone: in the middle a large two-light window holds Christ blessing, flanked by two archangels trampling monsters, and three glazed bowls made in the Arab world are set into the wall above them.

The way back turns north through the vineyards towards Berzano, the leaves already turning, and over Cinzano and Sciolze, on white roads between green fields under big clouds.
