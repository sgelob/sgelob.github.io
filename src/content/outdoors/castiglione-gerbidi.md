Six and a half kilometres and 71 metres of climbing, a winter walk down to the plain along the Strada dei Gerbidi and back up by the old lanes, in December.

The day was clear and cold, and from the lanes on the plain the whole chain of the Alps stood white above the bare trees and the fields.
