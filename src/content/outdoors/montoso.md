Nearly three kilometres and 74 metres of climbing around Montoso, a hamlet of Bagnolo Piemonte at almost 1,300 metres, at the end of May.

A short walk on the slopes above the Valle Infernotto, along the panoramic road and the quarry road, past clearings of gravel between firs and birches in the first heat of the season. Montoso is quarry country: these mountains are part of the district of the Pietra di Luserna, the grey-green stone of so many Piedmontese roofs and pavements, and some of the quarries around the hamlet are still worked.
