Seventeen kilometres and 81 metres of climbing on foot, a flat walk from Settimo along the plain of the Po to Gassino and back, on a grey day in October.

The way follows the bank of the Cimena canal and the farm tracks between the ploughed fields, with the mist hiding the hills, then turns back along the gravel roads at the foot of the slope, past rows of trees smothered in creepers.
