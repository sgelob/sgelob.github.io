Sixty-five kilometres and 1,143 metres of climbing on the cross-country bike, east across the hills into the Asti side of the Monferrato as far as Montafia, and back by another way.

The way out runs through Pavarolo, Montaldo and Marentino on small roads and farm tracks, past a fallow deer watching from behind a fence, to the Becchi, the hamlet of Castelnuovo where John Bosco was born in 1815. His mother, Margherita Occhiena, came from Capriglio, the next village along the route: she was born there in 1788 and moved to the Becchi when she married in 1812.

Beyond Capriglio the hills open into farmland round Montafia: gravel tracks between the maize and the stubble, vineyards with the grapes already dark, and lanes sunk deep into the woods, one of them blocked by a landslide and fallen trees.

The way back crosses the farmland past Buttigliera, Arignano and Andezeno, with a lone tree shading the track between the maize and a domed church far off in the haze, before the last climb over the hill by Baldissero and Cordova.
