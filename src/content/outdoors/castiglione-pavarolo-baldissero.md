Twenty-nine kilometres and 1,414 metres of climbing on the cross-country bike, on the hill between Gassino, Cordova, Pavarolo and Baldissero: short, steep climbs one after another, almost fifty metres up for every kilometre.

The route climbs over towards Gassino and Cordova, drops into the valleys around Tetti Francesi and Tetti Varetto, climbs again to Tetti None and loops east past Pavarolo before the last ridge above Baldissero.

March in the woods: bare trees, ivy climbing every trunk, sunken paths between banks of dry leaves, and in the damp hollows the first green of wild garlic. The winter had left its mark too. Trees had come down across the trails, one of them a great mossy trunk wrapped in ivy, lying right across the path.
