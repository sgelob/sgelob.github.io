Eight kilometres and 259 metres of climbing on foot, a short loop over the hill above Castiglione in August.

The way climbs along a gravel track into the woods and up a stony gully, past the dry bed of a stream and a fallen trunk lying across the water, then on narrow paths under leaning trees and across a shaded meadow.

Higher up the lanes pass an old stone house and a road runs along the hillside, with a vineyard on the slope, the plain spread out below and the wooded hills rolling away with a village on top.
