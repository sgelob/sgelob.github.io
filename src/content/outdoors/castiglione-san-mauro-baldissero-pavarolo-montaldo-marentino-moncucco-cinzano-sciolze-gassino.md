Fifty-three kilometres and 1,643 metres of climbing on the cross-country bike, out across the hills to the east as far as Moncucco and Cinzano, on the edge of the Monferrato, and back the long way round.

The day was clear enough to see the whole chain of the Alps. Above San Mauro the Torre di Moncanino stands over the woods with Monviso white behind it, and from there the route climbs past Rivodora and through Baldissero and Pavarolo, on cracked lanes and a cobbled stretch by a brick wall looking out over the hills.

Beyond Montaldo the hills open into farmland: gravel tracks past greenhouses, dirt roads between freshly sown fields, and Marentino on its hilltop across the green. Then tunnels of greenery on narrow paths, and long straight dirt roads between ploughed fields towards Moncucco.

Moncucco’s castle is first recorded in 1164; the present building is mostly fourteenth and fifteenth century, and the village has owned it since 1855. It now holds a museum of gypsum, quarried in these hills since the seventeenth century and worked into the decorated ceilings of the old houses of the Basso Monferrato.

From Cinzano the vineyards roll away towards the snowy Alps along the whole horizon, and the way back runs through Sciolze and over Bardassano to Gassino.
