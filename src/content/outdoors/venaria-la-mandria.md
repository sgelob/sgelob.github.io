Thirteen kilometres and 92 metres of climbing by bike, a loop from Venaria into the park of La Mandria, in April.

The gravel roads run straight through the light woods and along wooden fences, with the mountains still snowy above the meadows. By a farm a post of painted signs points to the animals, and a pony was grazing under the trees.

The castle of La Mandria was begun in 1708, when Victor Amadeus II had a stud farm built here to breed horses for the court of Venaria. In 1863 it became the private residence of Victor Emmanuel II, who came here to hunt and to live, away from the court, with Rosa Vercellana, la Bela Rosin, who later became his wife.
