Seventeen kilometres and 828 metres of climbing on foot, from Bousson, above Cesana in the upper Val di Susa, up to the Capanna Mautino and back, in the middle of July.

The way follows the long gravel road towards the Lago Nero, climbing through forests of larch and pine with the pale limestone peaks rising above the dark trees. Higher up the forest opens onto grassy slopes and a small lake among the larches.

The hut stands at about 2,100 metres in the Monti della Luna group, a short climb above the Lago Nero and its small chapel, the Madonna del Lago Nero, according to Planetmountain and Gulliver. From there the paths go on to other lakes and to the Colle Bercia, but this time the way turned back down to Bousson.

Four years earlier, in July 2020, the same walk had gone up the same way: thirteen kilometres and 856 metres of climbing, through the larch woods along a stream running over boulders, past huts in green pastures below the peaks and a house beside a small lake reflecting the clouds.
