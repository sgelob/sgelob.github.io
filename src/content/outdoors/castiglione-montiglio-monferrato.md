Seventy-six kilometres and 1,414 metres of climbing on the cross-country bike, a long loop out across the hills of the Monferrato to Montiglio and back along the Po, in October.

The way goes east over the hills by Sciolze, Rivalba and Cinzano, with mist lying in the valleys below and the woods turning yellow and copper. Past Albugnano, the hilltop village above its autumn vineyards, it drops towards Vallana on muddy tracks through the woods, over streams and up grassy double tracks across the meadows, then on through Passerano, Cerreto d’Asti and Piovà Massaia, with villages standing on every hilltop in the haze and a quarry cut into the side of a hill.

At Montiglio the road climbs to the old village, the Rocca, with its cobbled lanes and the castle above. It is among the largest and oldest castles of the Monferrato, and inside it the small chapel of Sant’Andrea keeps a cycle of frescoes from the middle of the fourteenth century, which the regional tourist board counts among the most important of that century in Piedmont.

The way back crosses the high fields north of Montiglio, freshly ploughed and pale under the sky, drops through the woods above Cavagnolo, on paths covered in leaves and down a flight of wooden steps, to the Po, and runs west along the river by Monteu da Po and Castagneto.
