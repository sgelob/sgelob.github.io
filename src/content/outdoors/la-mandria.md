Fifteen kilometres and 97 metres of climbing by bike, a loop through the park of La Mandria, beside Venaria, in June, with a storm building over the meadows.

The way runs on long straight gravel roads across the meadows and along cobbled avenues under tall trees, with the red brick buildings of the old estate at the end of them. Over the fields, a curtain of rain was coming down from the dark clouds.

La Mandria was the royal hunting estate of the House of Savoy. In the middle of the nineteenth century it was closed by a wall about 30 kilometres long, which still runs round it. In 1978 the Piedmont Region made it a park, one of the first regional parks in Italy.
