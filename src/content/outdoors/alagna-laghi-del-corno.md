Thirteen kilometres and 1,250 metres of climbing on foot, from above Alagna up to the Laghi del Corno and back, at the end of July.

The way climbs from the Alpe Priero on steep green slopes, past cows grazing above the valley, with the valleys fading into the haze below. Higher up the glaciers and the snowy peaks of Monte Rosa appear above the pastures, and at the top small lakes reflect them.

Alagna is a Walser village. Settlers from the north came over the passes in the thirteenth century, and in the hamlet of Pedemonte a Walser house of 1628, kept as it was, is now a museum of their life, according to the Turin tourist board.
