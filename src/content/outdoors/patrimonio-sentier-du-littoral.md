Nine kilometres and 152 metres of climbing on foot, on the coastal path along the Gulf of Saint-Florent below Patrimonio, at dawn, in August.

The path climbs from a pebble beach onto the slope above the vineyards and runs along the cliffs, on steps cut into the limestone and over layered rock, with the gulf and the mountains turning pink in the first light. Below, the white rock has been carved by the sea into arches, clefts and coves of turquoise water. Further on the way follows lanes along the shore and pebble beaches strewn with dried seagrass, then a long curve of sand round the bay.

The vineyards on the slopes are those of Patrimonio, the first wine appellation in Corsica, recognised in 1968. Its emblematic grape is the Niellucciu, and the appellation spreads over seven villages round the gulf.
