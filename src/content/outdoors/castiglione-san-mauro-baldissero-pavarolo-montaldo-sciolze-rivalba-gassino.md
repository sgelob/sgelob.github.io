Fifty-five kilometres and 1,715 metres of climbing on the cross-country bike, east across the hills between the Po and the Monferrato, where the road never stays level for long.

First over to San Mauro and up through Rivodora to the ridge above Baldissero, then east past Pavarolo and Montaldo on small roads, the tarmac cracked and the verges full of late-summer grass.

Near Sciolze the castle of Bardassano comes into view on its hill, with the basilica of Superga far behind it. Bardassano is first recorded in 1290, when it passed to the commune of Chieri and became one of its strongholds against the Marquis of Montferrat.

Beyond Sciolze the route drops into the woods of Mondesio and climbs out of them again and again, on dirt and gravel, past meadows where white cows grazed under the fruit trees.

Rivalba still has the octagonal tower of its castle, said to have been built early in the eleventh century by Landolfo, bishop of Turin, and today the village is known for its white truffle fair. Above Bussolino the clouds were gathering over the hills, and the last stretch ran down through Gassino and back.
