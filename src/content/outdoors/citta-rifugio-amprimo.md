Seven kilometres and 314 metres of climbing on foot, a winter walk in the snow from Città, a hamlet high above San Giorio on the south side of the Susa valley, up to the Rifugio Amprimo and back.

The way up follows a forest track and old lanes between stone walls, buried in fresh snow, through bare woods of beech and birch and then larches, with the sun low between the trunks and the white mountains across the valley showing through the branches.

The Rifugio Onelio Amprimo stands at 1,385 metres on the Pian Cervetto, in the Orsiera Rocciavrè natural park, and belongs to the Bussoleno section of the CAI. In September 1944 Nazi and Fascist troops hunting partisans in these woods burnt it to the ground. Walkers on Komoot write that the path up from Città is fine in winter snow with microspikes.

From the clearings near the hut the Rocciamelone stands white across the valley, 3,538 metres high. Bonifacio Rotario of Asti is said to have been the first to climb it, in 1358, carrying up a brass triptych of the Virgin, and since 1899 a bronze Madonna has stood on its summit.
