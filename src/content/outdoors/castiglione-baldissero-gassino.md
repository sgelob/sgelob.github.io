Thirty kilometres and 1,251 metres of climbing on the cross-country bike, south over the hill to Baldissero and back by Pavarolo and Gassino, with mist in the woods and a clear blue sky above the ridge.

Most of it is single track in winter woods: trails along steep banks deep in dry leaves, a fallen trunk across the path, a waymarked tree in the mist, and in one damp valley the first leaves of wild garlic already pushing up. Higher up the slopes, between the bare trunks, the plain showed far off below.

The highest part of the ride follows path 54 above Baldissero, towards the Bric della Croce Bianca. Riders who share it on Komoot describe it as a pleasant trail between Baldissero and the cross, with a steep final pitch up to it, and the climb comes out into the sun on a dirt road across a clearing of dry grass.

The way back over Pavarolo and down towards Gassino is a different story: farm tracks churned by tractor tyres, puddles in every rut, and sunken lanes between banks of ivy, the kind of mud that stays all winter in these hills.
