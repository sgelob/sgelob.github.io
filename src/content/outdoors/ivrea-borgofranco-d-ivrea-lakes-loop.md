Eighteen kilometres and 482 metres of climbing on foot, a loop from Lago Sirio through the morainic hills north of Ivrea to Borgofranco and back past the lakes.

The hills here are the moraines left by the great glacier that came down the Aosta valley, and in their hollows lie five small glacial lakes: Sirio, Pistono, Nero, San Michele and Campagna. Since 2024 they have been protected together as the natural park of the Cinque Laghi d’Ivrea. The way out passes the first of them, still and blue below wooded slopes, then climbs through spring woods to rocky outcrops where the lakes show far below, set among the trees under the snowy mountains of the Aosta valley.

From there paths cut through the rock, past a flooded wood and an old mossy wall, along clear streams, and down through oak and birch woods to the valley floor near Borgofranco, with a waterfall rushing through a narrow gorge and wild garlic thick under the trees. Lago Nero lies at about 300 metres, between Montalto Dora and Borgofranco.

After the cobbled squares and streets of Borgofranco the way back crosses meadows yellow with flowers and grassy tracks through the fields. The castle of Montalto Dora stands on its wooded hill, Monte Crovero, above Lago Pistono, and the last stretch follows a cobbled lane between stone walls and pillars back to the lakes.
