Ten and a half kilometres and 225 metres of climbing on foot, a short loop over the first slopes of the hill above Castiglione and back along the plain by the Po.

The way up goes into the Valle Garavaglia through a wood of thin trunks and ivy, and climbs on a sunken path under arching trees, past a boulder furred with moss. Higher up, by Tetti Varetto, the trees open onto meadows and the green hills folding away towards the east, and from the edge of the slope the town lies below with the wooded hill rising behind it.

The descent follows narrow lanes sunk between leafy banks, with the basilica of Superga appearing on its hill beyond the trees. Down on the plain a gravel track runs between the maize and the meadows, Superga now only a small shape on the horizon, and the walk ends through a young wood and a tunnel of green.
