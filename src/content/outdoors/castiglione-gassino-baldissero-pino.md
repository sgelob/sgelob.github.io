Thirty-seven kilometres and 1,600 metres of climbing on the cross-country bike, across the whole hill from Gassino to Pino and back, on a grey day at the end of winter.

The first part follows path 82 through the woods above Gassino and Bussolino, past stacks of cut logs, fallen trees and an old wooden hut half hidden among the trunks, then the Strada della Trinità over to Bardassano, where a dry meadow opens towards the hills.

From Pavarolo the route climbs west to the ridge between Baldissero and Pino. The woods there were being cut: a forestry works sign and tape across the trees, branches strewn over the tracks, and deep ruts in the mud. Along the Via dei Colli and down towards Rivodora the paths ran through bare woods, deep in last year’s leaves.

On the way back, on a wooded hilltop near Cordova, stands a small votive shrine, a pilone. They are everywhere in the Piedmontese countryside, at crossroads and along old paths: many date from the fifteenth century, and around Turin local tradition often ties them to the plagues of the early seventeenth century.
