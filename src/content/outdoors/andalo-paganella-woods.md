Twelve and a half kilometres and 476 metres of climbing on foot, a loop through the forests on the slopes of the Paganella above Andalo, in August.

The way climbs on stony tracks through the spruce forest, among tall straight trunks and upturned roots, with the peaks of the Brenta glimpsed between the trees. Higher up it comes out on clearings and meadows facing the jagged peaks under big white clouds, and comes back down to the village on forest tracks and a boardwalk along the meadows.
