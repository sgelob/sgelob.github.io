Ten kilometres and 469 metres of climbing on foot, from San Mauro by the Po straight up the hill to the basilica of Superga and back down the other side.

We left from the centre of San Mauro and climbed through green summer woods, on stony paths between hazel and young trees, past Moncanino to the top of the hill. The last stretch is an old cobbled path that comes out under the walls of the basilica.

From the terrace the whole city lay below, with the Alps half hidden in the summer cloud. The way down follows the woods on the San Mauro side through Sant’Anna, on stony paths through the oaks and past a broken footbridge, to the river, where a heron was wading in the shallows below the bridge.
