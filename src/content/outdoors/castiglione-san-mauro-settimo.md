Twenty kilometres and 116 metres of climbing on the plain below the hill, by bike to San Mauro and Settimo and back, in May.

The way runs on farm tracks and the paths along the canals, past bamboo hedges and open fields, with the Po never far. From Sambuy it follows the bank of the Cimena canal towards Settimo and comes back across the plain the same easy way.
