Nearly six kilometres and 43 metres of climbing, a short loop on the northern edge of Swindon in March.

The way climbs gently from Redhouse to St Andrews Ridge and back down by Tadpole Lane, through the new estates and the green spaces left between them. From the top of the ridge a wide field of wet grass runs down to a long row of houses, under a heavy grey sky that kept threatening and never quite rained.
