Seventeen kilometres and 594 metres of climbing on foot, a long loop over the hill from Castiglione to Pavarolo and round by Baldissero.

The way climbs past Cordova on narrow roads and gravel tracks under the trees, then drops into the gullies towards Pavarolo: paths sunk between banks of ivy, a big oak spreading over a path of roots, a muddy gully under arching branches and a trail beside a stream. Below the village the country opens into meadows under a wooded hill with a pale scar of rock, and the road climbs past the village sign with Pavarolo and its tower standing on the hilltop.

From there the route turns south and west through the fields towards Baldissero, then up path 54, on a lane between hedges, through light woods and a meadow of tall grass, with a church and the houses of the hill standing out above the plain and the valleys showing through gaps in the leaves.

The last part winds back north through the woods past Tetti None, on paths sunk between wooded banks, under leaning reeds and through a thin wood of slender trunks.
