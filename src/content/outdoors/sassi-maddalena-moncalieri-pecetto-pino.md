Forty-five kilometres and 1,427 metres of climbing on the cross-country bike, from Sassi by the Po over the Turin hill to Moncalieri, and back along the ridge by Pecetto and Pino.

On the way up by Mongreno and Cento Croci the trails were deep in fallen leaves, lit by a low sun, and higher up, past Pietra del Gallo, the meadows were white with frost and a thin layer of snow lay on the leaves.

At the Maddalena the snow still covered the grass, and below it the whole city lay spread out under a clear sky, with the Alps white from end to end of the horizon. From there we went south over the hill towards Moncalieri, on gravel lanes and leafy paths down to Testona, where umbrella pines stand over the plain. Its parish church, Santa Maria di Testona, goes back to the eleventh century; it was rebuilt in the Piedmontese Baroque, but, as a Komoot highlight notes, the old bell tower survives.

The way back climbs again by Santa Brigida and Rocciamelone, a hamlet of Moncalieri that shares its name with the mountain, to the ridge at Pecetto, then north past Pino and the Via dei Colli, before the long descent on the Strada del Cartman to Sassi.
