Fifteen kilometres and 239 metres of climbing on foot, two days before Christmas, from Settimo across the plain and up onto the first slopes of the hill, by San Martino and Sambuy, and back.

The day was clear and cold, and from the hillside the snowy Alps stood along the whole horizon above the bare winter woods. Between the trees a bell tower rose from its wooded hill, and houses sat on the slopes with the white mountains behind them.
