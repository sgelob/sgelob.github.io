Six kilometres and 91 metres of climbing on foot, a short loop above Gressoney-Saint-Jean to the Castel Savoia and back through the larch woods, at the end of June.

The way leaves the village past wooden houses with balconies full of flowers and vegetable plots, with the snowy peaks of Monte Rosa at the head of the valley, and climbs gently to the castle on its terrace above the valley. It comes back on paths through the larches, past a waterfall pouring over grey rock.

The Castel Savoia was built between 1899 and 1904 for Queen Margherita, who had fallen for the valley on her stays here. It is an eclectic summer villa with five towers, designed by the architect Emilio Stramucci, and the Aosta Valley region has owned it since 1981.
