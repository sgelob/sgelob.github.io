Five and a half kilometres and 23 metres of climbing along the Po in the south of Turin, on a bright day at the end of March.

The walk starts in Nizza Millefonti, near the grounds built for Italia ’61, the exhibition that marked a hundred years of Italian unity, and goes down to the river. The first trees were in blossom, white against a clear sky.

The path follows the bank under the bridges, with the hill on the other side, up and down earth steps under the trees and along a gravel track between a fence and the willows. In one stretch the bank was a building site, then the path opened again onto the river, wide and slow, with one big cloud over the hill.
