Nearly forty-two kilometres and only 84 metres of climbing, a flat ride across the plain south-west of Turin, from Airasca to Moretta and back, in June.

Most of the way out runs on the Via delle Risorgive, a cycle path laid on the bed of the old Airasca to Saluzzo railway, which lost its passenger trains in 1986. It is a straight ribbon of asphalt with a dashed line down the middle, between hay meadows, rows of trees and fields of young maize, through Scalenghe and Vigone. The whole path from Airasca to Moretta is about nineteen kilometres, and it is meant to go on towards Saluzzo as part of the EuroVelo 8 route.

The way back left the old railway at Moretta and came back by country roads through Villafranca Piemonte, then Vigone and Scalenghe again, across a plain so flat that the whole ride climbed less than a hundred metres.
