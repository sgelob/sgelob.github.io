Ten kilometres and 273 metres of climbing on foot, a loop over the hills towards Cordova and Tetti Varetto, in November.

The woods were at the height of their colours, yellow and copper against a clear blue sky, and from the open slopes the city and the plain lay below the Alps, white with snow. The way follows quiet lanes through the autumn woods and then sunken paths deep in fallen leaves, past a waymark post, and comes back down on a lane under yellow trees.
