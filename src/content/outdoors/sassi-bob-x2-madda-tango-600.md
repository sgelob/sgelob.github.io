Thirty-seven kilometres and 1,280 metres of climbing on the trail bike, from Sassi by the Po up onto the ridge of the Turin hill and south to the Maddalena. The title is a list of the trails: Bob, ridden twice, the Maddalena, Tango, and path 600 back down. Riders on Komoot call Bob fairly rough but great fun, with demanding climbs.

The way up follows the Strada del Cartman from the river to the Via dei Colli, then runs south along the ridge past Monte Aman and Pino. Pino has the hill’s observatory and, since 2007, Infini.to, a planetarium and space museum.

Beyond Pino the trails drop into the woods below the Eremo and climb back to the top of the day, about 715 metres near the Colle della Maddalena. In mid-April the woods were only just coming into leaf: thin trunks in a haze of pale green and white petals over the single track.

The way back runs north again through Cento Croci and Monte Aman, before the long descent on the Strada del Cartman to Sassi.
