Thirty-one kilometres and 1,259 metres of climbing on the trail bike, much of it on dirt in the woods of the hill between Castiglione, Rivodora, Pavarolo and Gassino. The 76 in the title is the number of a waymarked path, and Tanohill the name of a trail.

From Cordova the route drops into the Valle Garavaglia and works its way west towards San Martino and Rivodora, on trails worn deep into the soft ground: a path along an ivy-covered bank, a gully filled with green, a rut so deep the roots stand out on both sides, and a cattle grid on a gravel path below the woods.

In April the floor of the woods was white with wild garlic in flower, the single track only a narrow line through it. The plant carpets damp, shady woods in spring, and its Italian name, aglio orsino, bear’s garlic, comes from the old belief that bears seek it out when they wake from their winter sleep.

Back near Cordova, the ridge path marked in red and white passes an old boundary stone half buried in the leaves. Then east over the hill past Pavarolo and down through the woods towards Gassino.
