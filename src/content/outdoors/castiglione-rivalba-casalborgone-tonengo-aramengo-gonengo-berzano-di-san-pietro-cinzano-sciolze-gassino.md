Fifty-seven kilometres and 1,531 metres of climbing on the cross-country bike, east over the hills into the Asti side of the Monferrato as far as Tonengo and Aramengo, and back through Berzano, Cinzano and Sciolze.

From the road over Bardassano the valleys below were full of mist, with a castle standing on the ridge above it, and beyond the fields Rivalba rose out of the haze round its castle.

Past Casalborgone the route climbs into the hills of Tonengo, on trails deep in fallen leaves and gravel tracks along the vineyards, with the snowy Alps along the whole horizon. Tonengo and its neighbour Moransengo have been a single municipality since the start of 2023, about four hundred people between them. Just beyond, Gonengo is one of the many hamlets scattered over the hills of Aramengo, a handful of red roofs above a wooded valley.

The way back turns west through Berzano di San Pietro and Cinzano, and over the last ridges by Sciolze and Gassino.
