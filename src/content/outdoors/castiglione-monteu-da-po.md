Fifty-six kilometres and 1,353 metres of climbing on the cross-country bike, east along the hills above the Po as far as Monteu da Po, and back across the plain by the river.

Fog lay over the hill, and the first dirt, on path 82 above Gassino, was all mud: tractor ruts along the fields and sandy tracks fading into the grey. Past Bussolino and Rivalba the trails ran through bare woods deep in fallen leaves, past a wooden hut among the trees.

Beyond Casalborgone and Civignola the hills showed what the autumn rain had done. A lane had broken away behind an orange barrier, and fallen trees, one of them draped in ivy, arched over the paths. Then grassy tracks along the vineyards round Piazzo, with a village on its hilltop above the wooded slopes.

The route reaches the plain at the foot of Monteu da Po, near where the Roman town of Industria stood by the river. Pliny the Elder records it, under its older name Bodincomagus, the market on the Po, and it is known above all for its sanctuary of Isis and Serapis, rare in the north of Italy.

The way back runs west through Lauriano and up again into the hills of San Sebastiano and Castagneto, where whole slopes had slid away into raw scars of earth, then down to the plain by San Raffaele, on gravel roads between the fields, past a Christmas tree built of hay bales standing in a field.
