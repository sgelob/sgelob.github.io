Nine kilometres and 111 metres of climbing on foot, from Andalo across the meadows to Cavedago and back, in August.

The lanes wind through green meadows below the forest, past wooden farmhouses and a tree trunk carved into figures, with the mountains half hidden in the clouds. Cavedago is a village of masi, the old farmsteads of the plateau, and on the way a street passes under a stone arch. The way back runs through the pine woods.

At the end, below Andalo, lies the lake that is not always there. According to the Trentino tourist board, the lake of Andalo has no stream flowing into it: it is fed by the basin around it and by a karst system underground, which can raise its level within a few days, and in summer it is practically dry, a wide meadow below the mountains.
