Thirteen kilometres and 467 metres of climbing on foot, over the hill above Gassino and down into the fields at Pedaggio, below Bussolino.

The way climbs past a green gate onto the Strada della Trinità and into the woods above Gassino, on rutted tracks and lanes under tall trees, past a trail sign at a fork and a stack of old stone blocks beside the path. The woods were in full spring green, with a dead tree standing pale among them, ivy climbing the trunks and meadows of tall grass opening at their edges, the hills showing beyond.

Path 82 then drops through narrow gullies, sunk deep between leafy banks, towards Bussolino and the open country at its foot: a road through the meadows towards a village on its hill, sandy tracks forking across the fields and a gravel track between a ploughed field and the grass.

The climb back goes into the woods again, where big leaves crowded the path and a frog sat in a muddy pool, and the last stretch comes back over the top, with the green hillside and its houses falling away to the hazy plain.
