Forty-five kilometres and 1,157 metres of climbing on the cross-country bike, a winter loop over the Turin hill from Sassi, twice up from the river. The title is a list of the trails and places: path 600, the Parco Leopardi, the Maddalena, the Monte dei Cappuccini and the Val San Martino.

The first climb goes up by Mongreno to the Via dei Colli, where frost still lay white on the trails along the ridge. From Monte Aman the basilica of Superga showed across the valley above its wooded slopes, before the descent on the Strada del Cartman back to Sassi.

Then along the Po into the city and up again from the Parco Leopardi through San Vito to the Maddalena, where patches of snow lay in the meadows of the Parco della Rimembranza and the whole chain of the Alps stood white beyond the plain.

The way down ends at the Monte dei Cappuccini, just above the river. The Capuchin friars built their convent there at the end of the sixteenth century, on land given by Duke Charles Emmanuel I; the city took it over in 1871, and since 1874 it has held the Club Alpino’s mountain museum. From its terrace we looked over the rooftops to the Mole Antonelliana, with the snowy mountains behind it, before the last climb and the Val San Martino back down to Sassi.
