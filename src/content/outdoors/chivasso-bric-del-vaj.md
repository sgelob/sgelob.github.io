Thirteen kilometres and 547 metres of climbing on foot, from the bridge over the Po at Chivasso up into the hills of Castagneto Po to the Bosco del Vaj and back, at the end of April.

The way crosses the river under heavy clouds and climbs straight into the wood on steps and narrow paths among the ferns. Along the Strada San Genesio the road passes a massive Romanesque bell tower: according to the municipality, the first church here was built between 1019 and 1150 by the abbey of Fruttuaria, and the bell tower is the best preserved part of it. Higher up the path runs through an unexpected grove of tall bamboo and past a palm, before the wooded hills open up in the haze.

The Bosco del Vaj has been a regional nature reserve since 1978, a wood of beech, oak and chestnut on the hills above the Po. Regional tourism material notes that its soft rocks have yielded fossils of plants and animals since the eighteenth century. The paths go up and down through the beeches, over a wooden bridge and past a stream running over pebbles, to a viewpoint with a panorama board and a picnic area in a clearing.

The way back comes down by Cimenasco, past another church and bell tower, along lanes and grassy tracks to the bright green fields at the foot of the hill, and back across the plain to the river.
