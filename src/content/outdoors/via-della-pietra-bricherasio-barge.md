Twenty-six kilometres and 74 metres of climbing by bike, from Bricherasio to Barge and back on the Via della Pietra, the cycle path along an old railway line at the foot of the mountains, on a clear September morning.

The line was planned in 1877, when a private consortium had a branch drawn up from the Pinerolo – Torre Pellice railway to Barge; it was just under twelve kilometres long and was closed by decree in 1984, according to the Piedmont Region. Today the old track bed makes a cycle path almost perfectly flat.

It crosses the wide stony bed of the Pellice on a long bridge and runs straight between meadows, orchards and young vineyards, with the mountains hazy in the morning light, and a rocky hill rising above the fields of maize. Small old buildings stand here and there beside the path.

At Barge the way leaves the cycle path for the cobbled streets of the old town, past a ruined tower above a stream and a small chapel on a cobbled square, and then goes back the same way, past rows of cut hay below the mountains.
