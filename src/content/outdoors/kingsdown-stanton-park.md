Three kilometres and 16 metres of climbing on foot, a short loop from Kingsdown into Stanton Park, in March, when the paths were more mud than path.

The way follows a boardwalk into the wood and then a track along a flooded patch of trees, churned into deep mud. It comes out by the lake, where the water lay still and the clouds were reflected between the trees.

According to Swindon Borough Council, the park grew out of a medieval estate and was landscaped in the nineteenth century, with the lake and the old woods; it hides the remains of a Roman villa, with a bath house and mosaic floors from around 200 AD, though nothing shows above the ground. Since 2003 the woods and the lake have been a local nature reserve.
