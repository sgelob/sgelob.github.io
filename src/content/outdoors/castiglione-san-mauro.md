Ten kilometres and 331 metres of climbing on foot, a loop over the slopes between Castiglione and San Mauro in September.

The way climbs past a lone oak on a grassy top, with the new houses of the hillside below and the wooded hills beyond the meadows. Then it drops into the woods, on paths half hidden by the undergrowth, through a cane brake and past a rusting old machine left among the trees.

Lower down the paths follow a stream over pebbles, under a wall of rock, to a small waterfall falling into a stony gully, before the last stretch through the woods.
