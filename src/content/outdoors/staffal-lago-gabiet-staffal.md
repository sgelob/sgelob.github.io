Two kilometres and 67 metres of climbing at about 2,300 metres, a short walk to the Lago Gabiet above Gressoney-La-Trinité, in July 2020.

The way follows a gravel track up a green valley below jagged peaks with patches of snow, past a hut under a grassy summit, to the lake held back by its dam. Big clouds were building over the mountains, and the water was high and pale blue. Four years later the same lake would be almost empty for work on the dam.
