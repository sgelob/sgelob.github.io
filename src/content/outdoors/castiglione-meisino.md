Twenty-five kilometres and 91 metres of climbing, a flat ride along the Po to the Meisino, where the Stura di Lanzo meets it at the edge of Turin, and back by San Mauro, in April.

Most of the way follows dirt paths on the embankments, here beside a long blue railing in the spring sun, out to the Ponte Diga and the reserve at the confluence, then back along the river through Sambuy.
