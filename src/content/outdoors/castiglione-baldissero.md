Thirty kilometres and 1,241 metres of climbing on the cross-country bike, round and round the hill between Castiglione, Cordova and the woods above Baldissero, where every valley means another climb out of it.

It works its way up and down the slopes around Tetti Varetto and Tetti Francesi, over to the edge of Pavarolo and up to the ridge above Baldissero, across a meadow past two old oaks and on narrow trails between dry grass and bare bushes.

It was still mostly winter in the woods: bare trunks, ivy on the ground, last year’s leaves deep on the paths, and a pile of freshly cut logs beside a track. Only on the floor of the damp valleys was spring under way, with the first leaves of wild garlic spreading green on both sides of the single track.
