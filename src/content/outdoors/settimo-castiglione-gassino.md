Seventeen kilometres and 432 metres of climbing on foot, from Settimo across the plain to the Po and up onto the hill above Gassino.

The first part is flat: out of Settimo and across the river, then along the north bank of the Cimena canal on a white gravel track through grass taller than the fences, with the hill rising ahead under a hazy sky.

Past the gravel works the way leaves the plain and climbs into the hill, on lanes between ivy-covered walls and hedges and paths under tunnels of branches, up the Strada della Trinità above Gassino towards Bardassano. Up there the paths were dry and dusty after the summer heat, narrow trails along eroded banks through the scrub and hazel woods.
