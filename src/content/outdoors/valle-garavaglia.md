Seven kilometres and 358 metres of climbing on foot, a short loop through the Valle Garavaglia in November.

The valley was in its autumn colours, the meadows soft in the hazy light below the wooded hills. In the bare woods ivy had climbed over the boulders, an old wooden hut stood half hidden among the branches, and lower down horses grazed below the hill.
