Twelve kilometres and 462 metres of climbing on foot, up through the valleys above Castiglione to the cross on the Bric della Croce Bianca and back over Cordova.

The path drops into the Valle Garavaglia and follows the stream up its narrow wooded gorge, past the thin waterfall of the Rio Crivella and over banks of bare rock and roots, with the mud already cracked dry beside the trail. Above Tetti Francesi it leaves the wood for meadows of tall grass, passes under a natural arch of branches, and climbs back into the trees along a stream bed of rock slabs and still pools.

Beyond Tetti None the way climbs towards Cordova on faint paths through the grass, so tall in places that the track was only a narrow slot between two walls of it, then on a gravel track sunk between wooded banks. Path 51 leads on towards Baldissero and the top of the bric, where a large cross stands among the trees. According to a local geocache page, it is five and a half metres tall and was put up in 1900 for the new century, paid for by a collection among the people of Cordova, Baldissero and Pavarolo.

The way back runs down the road through the woods and along the slopes above Gassino, with a last dry meadow looking out over the wooded hills to the Alps.
