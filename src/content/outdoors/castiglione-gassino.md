Under eight kilometres and 250 metres of climbing on the cross-country bike, a short figure of eight on the hill between Castiglione and Gassino.

The loop climbs on the slopes towards Bardassano and comes back down through the woods, on a narrow dirt track under a tunnel of branches, the ground already dry and scattered with twigs.

At the top of the loop the trees open onto a field, with the castle of Bardassano on the wooded hill across it. Local tradition says Frederick Barbarossa stayed there in 1156, after his siege of Chieri, though no document backs the story up.
