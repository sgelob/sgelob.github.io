Nearly four kilometres and 35 metres of climbing, a short evening loop on the northern edge of Swindon in the middle of June.

The way crosses the open green spaces between the new estates around Redhouse: wide mown fields under a big sky, long grass left to grow at the edges, mown paths through the meadows to the trees. Between one field and the next the houses are never far, red brick with gables, a Union flag flying by one of them.
