A short walk of a kilometre and a half round the lake of Stanton Country Park, on the edge of Swindon, in June.

The path starts in the wood, where two old walls stand on either side of the way. They are among the last pieces of the estate: the house was left derelict and finally demolished, but parts of the old garden walls and the ha-ha are still there, according to a council leaflet. The lake was busy with ducks crowding the shore, swans by the walkway and reeds along the far bank, and beyond the trees sheep were grazing in front of a stone house.

On a walk in March the year before, the same paths had been all mud.
