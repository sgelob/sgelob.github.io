Forty-four kilometres and 1,422 metres of climbing on the cross-country bike, a big loop east along the hills above the Po to Castagneto and Casalborgone, and back over the ridges of Cinzano and Sciolze.

The first part follows path 82 through the woods above Gassino and Bussolino, with the snowy Alps lined up along the horizon beyond the bare orchards, then drops into the Valle Baudana on trails that winter had turned to mud: ruts between mossy banks, lanes deep in ivy, and an overgrown track down into the valley.

Above San Raffaele the view opens all the way to the basilica of Superga on its hill, with Monviso white behind it and the town spread out below. The route goes on by Via Pertengo, which local riders list on Trailforks among the best descents of the San Raffaele and Castagneto hill. That day the lane beyond was closed off with barriers, and further on its edge had slid away into the gully.

From Castagneto, on old lanes of cracked and patched tarmac, the way drops to Casalborgone and climbs back by the Strada Vecchia towards Cinzano, then along farm tracks between green winter fields towards Sciolze, the hills rolling away under a grey sky and the Alps faint behind them.
