Thirteen kilometres and 423 metres of climbing on foot, a loop over the hills above Gassino to Pavarolo and Bardassano, in July.

The way climbs between dry meadows and wooded knolls, then dives into the woods on paths down narrow gullies, where fallen branches and piles of cut wood blocked the way here and there. From the open slopes the plain stretched to the horizon, and the basilica of Superga stood on the skyline across the valley.

Past the fields of Pavarolo the way reaches Bardassano, with its red brick church on a paved square and a lane between old houses leading to the clock tower. The way back runs on gravel roads and shady lanes through dense green woods, past young vineyards on a slope and along grassy tracks between the bushes.
