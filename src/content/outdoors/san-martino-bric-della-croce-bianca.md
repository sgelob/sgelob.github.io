Eight kilometres and 287 metres of climbing on foot, a loop from San Martino over the wooded hills to the Bric della Croce Bianca and back by Tetti None, on the last day of April.

The woods had just come into leaf, a deep fresh green under a cloudy sky. The way starts by a modern church covered in ivy and goes into the trees on narrow paths through the undergrowth. Between the woods the basilica of Superga showed on its hill beyond a garden wall, and from the slopes a village sat above the wooded valleys with the Alps faint in the haze.

The path climbs through tall trunks and sunken lanes, past banks of layered rock under the ivy, to the cross among the trees on the top of the bric. The way back crosses meadows on grass tracks, fords a small stream in the wood and ends at a chapel with a picnic area by the road.
