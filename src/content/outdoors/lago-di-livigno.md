Fifteen kilometres on foot, almost flat, along the shore of the Lago di Livigno and back, in August.

The path follows the shore through the larch woods, with the turquoise water stretching away between steep mountains and rocky points reaching into the lake.

The lake is a reservoir, held back by the Punt dal Gall dam on the Swiss border. According to Wikipedia, the first filling began on 16 October 1968, and the water covered the floor of the valley, where a small church had to be pulled down.
