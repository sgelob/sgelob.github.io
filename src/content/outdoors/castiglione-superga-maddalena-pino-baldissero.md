Forty-five kilometres and 1,254 metres of climbing on the cross-country bike, along the whole ridge of the Turin hill from Superga to the Maddalena and back by Pino and Baldissero, all of it in fog.

The cloud never lifted. The trails through the woods were buried in yellow and brown leaves, and every path ran a few dozen metres ahead and then dissolved into grey.

At Superga the basilica appeared only as a portico of columns, the dome lost above it. On a clear day the 131 steps of the spiral stair up to the balcony round the dome give one of the widest views of Turin and the Alps; that day there was nothing to see but the fog itself. Along the Via dei Colli, past Cento Croci and the Eremo, it was the same, and at the Maddalena the bronze Victory of the lighthouse stood as a faint shape over the empty meadows.

The way back drops off the ridge by Pino into the Valle Miglioretti and the Valle Ceppi, and climbs over the hill past Baldissero and Pavarolo, on dirt trails through grey woods.
