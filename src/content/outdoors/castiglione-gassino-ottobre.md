Twelve kilometres and 374 metres of climbing on foot, over the hill between Castiglione and Gassino in October.

The woods were turning: paths deep in fallen leaves, a tree down across the way, mushrooms pushing up through the litter and a tall parasol standing on its own among the dead leaves. Between the woods the meadows opened onto the hills, with a big oak in the grass and the haze lying in the valleys.

From the slopes the basilica of Superga showed on its hill above the misty valleys, and lower down the lanes passed vineyards turning gold. The last stretch crosses the plain on a straight road towards the hill.
