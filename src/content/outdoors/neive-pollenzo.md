Fifty-six kilometres and 175 metres of climbing by bike, from Castagnito across the plain of the Tanaro past Alba and Roddi to Pollenzo and back, in July.

The way follows gravel roads across the fields and along the river, past poplar groves and ponds of reeds below the hills of the Langhe and the Roero, and the pale cliffs of the Roero above the fields.

Pollenzo was the Roman town of Pollentia, and the remains of its amphitheatre lie under the houses. In 1832 King Charles Albert bought the castle and remade the village in a neo-Gothic style, with a red brick tower and church, and built a model farm with vineyards and cellars. It has been a UNESCO World Heritage Site since 1997, with the other Savoy residences, and the farm now houses a university of food.
