Sixteen kilometres and 472 metres of climbing on foot, east along the hills above the Po from Gassino to San Raffaele Cimena and back along the foot of the hill.

The first part climbs out of Gassino on path 82 towards Bussolino, through hazel woods and along fields of sunflowers, their heads already drooping. Beyond Bussolino the paths follow stream beds of grey slabs and pebbles into the Valle Baudana, the hills hazy beyond.

From the slopes above San Raffaele the view opens over orchards and meadows to the plain of the Po, the town spread out in the valley below. The way back drops into the village and returns west through the Valle Baudana and along the foot of the hill to Gassino.
