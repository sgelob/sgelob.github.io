Fourteen kilometres and 585 metres of climbing on foot, from Andalo down to Lake Molveno and back over the slopes of Pradel, in August.

The way drops through the woods along a stream, over a wooden bridge and below walls of rock, past a waterfall falling into a green pool in a gorge, and comes out above Molveno, with the village below the jagged peaks of the Brenta and the lake an unreal turquoise between the mountains. The way back climbs to the meadows of Pradel, below the pines and the peaks, and returns to Andalo on a road through a tall spruce forest.

The lake was made by a landslide, which blocked the valley some three to four thousand years ago; it is the deepest natural lake in Trentino. When it was drained in 1951 for work on the hydroelectric plant, the trunks of a prehistoric forest appeared on its bed, about two hundred of them, according to the Touring Club.
