About eight kilometres and 520 metres of climbing on the second day: from Riomaggiore over the hill to Manarola, up through the vineyards to Volastra and along the high path to Corniglia. There is no recording of this day: the route is rebuilt from where the photos were taken.

The Via dell’Amore, the seaside path between Riomaggiore and Manarola, had been closed since a landslide in 2012 (it reopened only in 2024), so the way over went up the steep path behind Riomaggiore, through terraces where the whole coast opens towards Corniglia and Monterosso.

In Manarola an iron cross stands on the point above the waves, and the station sits on the cliff between two tunnels. A few photos of the village are from an earlier visit, in July 2014.

From Manarola to Volastra the path is mostly steps between dry-stone terraces of vines, with the small monorails that carry grapes and tools up the slopes running right beside it. After Volastra the trail turns into a balcony through vineyards and then woods, past a striped spider on a wall and a fallen pine across the way, until Corniglia comes into view below. The day ended with the sun going down into the sea.
