Nearly sixteen kilometres and 708 metres of climbing on foot, from Sant’Anna in San Mauro up through the natural park of the Superga hill to the ridge, along it towards Pino, and back down.

The walk starts up a lane painted blue and named the Sentiero delle Stelle, then climbs into the woods on narrow paths under fresh green leaves. Near the top the trees open onto the whole city: Turin spread out below, the little car of the rack railway that climbs from Sassi in the foreground, and the plain and the hills fading into the haze.

From Superga the route follows the Via dei Colli along the ridge under tall pines, past Monte Aman and through light oak woods, then turns down by the Strada del Cartman and Tetti Bertoglio. The paths there cross bare slabs of rock and drop through damp gullies, past a picnic shelter in a park of pines, and climb again by Tetti Rocco.

The way down runs past a lane between the maize and an old stone wall, through a deep gully under the trees, and back to the Po, where the cycle path follows the river to San Mauro.
