Forty-two kilometres and 1,371 metres of climbing on the cross-country bike, east along the hills above the Po to the two woods of Casalborgone and Castagneto, the Bosco del Vaj and the Bosc Grand, and back.

The way out follows path 82 past Gassino and Bussolino to Rivalba, where the trails had been churned into mud: deep ruts cut into the banks, sunken tracks between walls of earth, and stony gullies under bare trees still waiting for their leaves.

Past Casalborgone the route climbs to the ridges around San Sebastiano, on dirt roads with the plain in the haze below, and the sun came out over a valley of wooded hills with a village at the bottom. Towards Castagneto the track runs through a deep cutting in yellow sand.

The two woods are protected together as a site of community importance. The Bosc Grand, above Casalborgone, is known for the Ro Verda, a huge old oak of a rare kind, Quercus crenata, a cross between the Turkey oak and the cork oak.

The way back runs through the woods above San Raffaele and Rivalba, on single tracks where the first green shoots were coming up through the dry leaves.
