Seven and a half kilometres and 172 metres of climbing on foot, a first loop round Andalo, in August, under low clouds.

The way follows tracks along the edge of the woods, past a tractor, and the narrow lanes of the old village under an arch between the houses. Below the village lay the bed of the lake, dry and green at the end of summer, with the mountains lost in the clouds.
