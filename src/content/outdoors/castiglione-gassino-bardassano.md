Fifteen kilometres and 527 metres of climbing on foot, over the hill between Castiglione and Gassino and round the woods and meadows of Bardassano.

The first stretch looks out over the rooftops to the hazy hills, then goes down a flight of steps between a wall and tall reeds and on along narrow paths through a light wood to Gassino. Above the town the way climbs onto path 82 and comes out on a meadow of tall grass, with the wooded hills rolling away under the summer haze.

At Bardassano a lane runs beside a long wall with stone pillars under the trees. The hamlet’s castle dates back to the eleventh to thirteenth centuries, and in the seventeenth century part of its old defences were turned into garden terraces; it is still a private home. Round it the walk wanders through woods and meadows: a path under a pale bank of sandstone, a flowering meadow below a green hill, overgrown tracks through the bushes and a small pond hidden among the trees.

The last stretch follows the Strada della Trinità and the paths down through the woods.
