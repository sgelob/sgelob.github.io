Twelve kilometres by bike, flat, from Jesolo along the coast through the pine woods of Jesolo Pineta to Cortellazzo and back, in August.

The way follows cycle paths beside the roads, under rows of umbrella pines, and crosses a canal on a bridge full of flowers. At the far end it reaches the empty beach towards the mouth of the Piave, the sand pale under a grey sky.
