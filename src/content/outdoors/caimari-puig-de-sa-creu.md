Five kilometres and 470 metres of climbing on foot, a short, steep loop from Caimari to the Puig de sa Creu and back, two days before the walk to the Puig dels Albellons.

The path climbs straight out of the village through pines and scrub, on stony ground between tufts of tall grass. From the rocks near the top the whole plain of Mallorca opens below, with the sea on the far side of the island, while on the other side craggy grey peaks rise above deep wooded valleys: the heart of the Serra de Tramuntana.

The way down runs through an oak wood, past a wooden ladder leaning against a tree, and back to the village on a narrow road and gravel tracks under the oaks. Caimari sits right where the central plain meets the mountains, which is why a walk of barely five kilometres leaves it so quickly for the wild side of the island.
