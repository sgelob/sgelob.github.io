Forty kilometres and 881 metres of climbing on the cross-country bike, a loop from San Mauro by the Po: up over the hill to Superga, along the ridge towards Pino, and back down to the river through Turin.

San Mauro takes its old name, Pulcherada, from the Latin Pulchra Rada, the beautiful landing on the river. Its Benedictine abbey was rebuilt in 991 after the Saracens had destroyed it, and the church still keeps its twelfth-century bell tower.

From the river the route climbs past Tetti Varetto and along the Strada Bellavista to the ridge below Superga, then follows the Via dei Colli towards Monte Aman and Pino, with a loop down into the Valle Ceppi and back up. The trails along the ridge ran through woods still green, the first leaves only just starting to fall, and from the stony tracks near Monte Aman the basilica showed far off between the trees.

The way down drops by Tetti Rocco to Sassi, and the last stretch runs flat along the Po through the Meisino park back to San Mauro.
