Nine kilometres and 108 metres of climbing by bike, a loop round the Lago di Ceresole, at the end of June, under a cloudy sky.

The way runs round the lake past the village and over the dam, with snowy peaks above the water, through the larch woods past a waterfall and across a meadow where cows were lying in the grass.
