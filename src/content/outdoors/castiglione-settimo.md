Seventeen and a half kilometres and 266 metres of climbing on foot, out to Settimo and back on the same day in September, across the plain and along the edge of the hill.

It was one of those clear autumn days when the whole chain of the Alps stands along the horizon. The way out follows lanes along the foot of the slope, with the rooftops and the plain spread out below and the snowy mountains beyond, then crosses the plain on paths beside the main road.

The way back starts across a ploughed field with the hill of Superga rising ahead, climbs onto the slope with the town below and the Alps behind it, and follows shaded tracks and narrow paths along fences and hedges before the last stretch down through the trees.
