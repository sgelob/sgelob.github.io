Thirty-four kilometres and 387 metres of climbing by bike, a loop from Borgo San Dalmazzo along the rivers to Cuneo and back, at the beginning of July.

The way follows tracks through the river park, along the Stura and the Gesso to where they meet below the town, across meadows below the mountains and past the wide stony riverbed. It climbs into Cuneo to its great arcaded square and comes back through the fields.

Below the town the track passes under the Soleri viaduct, which carries road and railway on two levels across the valley of the Stura, about 800 metres long. The first stone was laid in 1913, the road opened in 1933 and the railway in 1937; after the war it was named after the politician Marcello Soleri.
