Six kilometres and 173 metres of climbing on the hill, up through the Valle Garavaglia to Tetti Francesi and back, in April 2021.

The path climbs through the woods of the valley, where in spring the ground is covered in wild garlic, then along the ridge by the farmhouses of Tetti Francesi and back down the other side of the valley.
