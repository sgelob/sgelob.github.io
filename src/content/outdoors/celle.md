Two kilometres and 66 metres of climbing around Celle, a hamlet of Caprie at about a thousand metres above the lower Val di Susa, in April.

A short stroll rather than a hike: past two donkeys in a meadow, along a path through woods still bare, and down a cobbled lane between old stone houses with flowerpots on the steps. Celle is where the trail to the Rocca Sella starts, behind the refuge in the hamlet, and from its edge the valley opens all the way to the plain.
