Forty-four kilometres and 1,606 metres of climbing on the cross-country bike, packed into the hills between Baldissero, the slopes above San Mauro and Gassino: short, steep climbs one after another, and nearly all of it on dirt.

It was a clear, cold day, with the leaves already down and lying deep on every trail. The first climbs go up by Cordova and Pavarolo, through oak woods striped with long shadows, to the ridge above the shaded valleys, the Alps white along the horizon.

From there the trails work west along the ridge by Tetti Pietraforata towards the side of the hill that drops to San Mauro: sunken paths between ivy-clad trees, fallen trunks across the way, a trail along a mossy bank above a gully, and the open hills round Baldissero with their farms and fields under a cloudless sky.

The last part crosses back over the hill past Bardassano and drops towards Gassino, where the plain of the Po opens up below with the mountains beyond it.
