Seven kilometres and 220 metres of climbing, a short summer loop on the mountain bike up the hill towards Tetti Francesi and Cordova and back, in July.

A quick one: steep lanes through the woods, and at the top a gap in the green with the plain and the far hills hazy in the heat.
