Ten and a half kilometres and 600 metres of climbing on foot, a winter loop from Crò up to Monte Freidour and round by the Rifugio Melano, on the first mountains above the plain west of Pinerolo.

It was a clear January day with the plain below drowned in a sea of haze, only the hills of Turin and the far Monferrato showing above it. The way climbs past a pasture and a hut, then through pines and bare beeches on rocky paths, past a tall rock standing beside the trail.

On the ridge the rocks break through in crests and knolls, and the view opens onto the Alps: dry brown ridges in front, white peaks behind, and Monviso rising above layers of blue valleys. Monte Freidour reaches about 1,445 metres.

The way down crosses slopes of boulders below rock walls and pinnacles, through bare woods and a tall pinewood, to the Rifugio Melano, Casa Canada, at about 1,060 metres, and back down to Crò.
