Six and a half kilometres and 146 metres of climbing on foot, a short loop from Dairin, above Talucco, to the Rifugio Melano Casa Canada at the foot of the Rocca Sbarua, at the beginning of September.

The way follows a quiet road and gravel tracks through woods of beech, birch and chestnut, past stone houses with wooden balconies, an old gateway onto a mule track and the twisted trunk of an old chestnut. Between the trees the valleys open up, and on the slopes the ferns were starting to turn.

The hut is a log house beside a big boulder, below the walls of the Rocca Sbarua. Planetmountain describes the crag as a wall of gneiss above Pinerolo where great names of Italian mountaineering climbed, and the favourite training ground of Giusto Gervasutti and his friends. The old hut closed in 2006, and the new one opened in September 2011, bringing climbers back to the rock.

The way back goes round the other side of the hill, on gravel tracks down through the trees, with the wooded hills fading into the haze and the rocky peaks rising above a village.
