Ten kilometres and 413 metres of climbing on foot, high above Prali at the head of the Val Germanasca: from the Bric Rond, where the chairlift from Pian dell’Alpet arrives, up to Passo Roux and round the plateau of the Tredici Laghi, the thirteen lakes, and down to Pian Alpet.

The walk starts at almost 2,500 metres, on a stony track along a grassy ridge, with the Alps fading away above a sea of cloud. The plateau of the lakes lies in a wide basin closed by the Cappello d’Envie, the Punta Cialancia and Punta Cournour: dry pasture and scree, and small lakes from pale white to deep blue scattered among the rocks.

From the lakes the path climbs over scree and broken slabs to Passo Roux, about 2,830 metres and the highest point of the day, where great tilted slabs of rock line the crest. On the other side the valleys were filled with cloud, and more cloud poured up the deep valley between the ridges.

The way down crosses the basin again, past a lake and the ruins of the old military barracks that still stand on the plateau, over slopes red with bilberry leaves, ripe berries among them, and down through the first larches to Pian Alpet.
