Thirteen kilometres and 689 metres of climbing on foot, from Pian del Re up to the Rifugio Quintino Sella below Monviso and back, in July.

Pian del Re is where the Po rises. From there the path climbs past a chain of lakes, from dark blue to turquoise, through rocky valleys and across patches of old snow, while the clouds came and went round the peaks of Monviso. Near the top the mist closed in over the scree and the dark water of the Lago Grande di Viso.

The hut stands beside that lake, at about 2,640 metres. The Italian Alpine Club opened it on 23 July 1905, and it is named after Quintino Sella, who made the first Italian ascent of Monviso in 1863 and is said to have conceived the club on its summit.
