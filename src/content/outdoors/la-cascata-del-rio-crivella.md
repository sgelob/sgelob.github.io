About five kilometres and 110 metres of climbing on the Turin hill, a short spring walk to the waterfall of the Rio Crivella, in April 2021.

The trees were still bare, but the ground had already turned green. The path follows the numbered trails of the hill through a sunken lane between rocky banks and along dirt tracks in the sun, past stacks of firewood at the edge of the wood.

Down in the valley the whole floor of the wood was covered in wild garlic, thick and bright under the bare trunks, and the path wound through it to the stream. The waterfall was running well, a white veil over the rock wall at the end of the gorge.
