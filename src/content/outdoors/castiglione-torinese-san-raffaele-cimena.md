Twenty-one kilometres and 81 metres of climbing by bike, a flat loop across the plain of the Po between Castiglione, Gassino and San Raffaele Cimena, in June.

The way runs mostly on farm tracks and gravel: it crosses the fields of ripe wheat under big summer clouds, a small wooden bridge leads into the trees, and long gravel tracks run between thick green hedges along the river plain.
