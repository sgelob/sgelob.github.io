Four kilometres on foot, almost flat, a walk round the centre of Ajaccio in the middle of August.

The way starts at the Place d’Austerlitz, where a wide stairway climbs to a statue of Napoleon on a stepped pyramid, in the uniform he wore at Austerlitz. It was unveiled in 1938, and beside it, under the rocks, is the grotto where the young Napoleon is said to have played, according to the Corsican tourist board.

From there the walk goes down through the streets to the old town, along a street hung with coloured bunting and narrow lanes between tall ochre houses, to a terrace with palms above the gulf.
