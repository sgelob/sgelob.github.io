Fifty-one kilometres and 1,382 metres of climbing on the cross-country bike, east across the hills into the Monferrato as far as Albugnano, and back through Moncucco and Marentino.

The valleys were full of fog, and from the ridge above Pavarolo only the hilltops rose out of it, the village with its castle and tower standing over a white sea. The castle is still a private house; in 1736 it passed to Alessandro Ferrero d’Ormea, who added a wing for the stables with the hayloft above.

Past Arignano and Mombello the frost still lay on the meadows, and the trails through the woods towards Moncucco ran over streams and under ivy and fallen branches. Then the climb past Vezzolano to Albugnano, where the air cleared and the whole chain of the Alps stood white along the horizon, above the bare vineyards.

These slopes make Albugnano’s own red, a Nebbiolo with its own appellation since 1997, grown only around Albugnano, Pino d’Asti, Castelnuovo Don Bosco and Passerano Marmorito.

The way back drops through the woods below Moncucco, on tracks running with water, and crosses Marentino and Montaldo, with Pavarolo on its hill and the mountains behind it.
