Eighteen kilometres and 600 metres of climbing on the trail bike, the last ride before leaving for the holidays: three, two, one. It also came to three climbs and three descents, short and steep, on the hills between Castiglione, Bardassano and Pavarolo.

The first climb goes up through Cordova on a quiet road under walls of ivy, and comes back down on a trail through the woods, already covered in dry leaves in the August heat. The second climbs past Bardassano to Pavarolo, and the third comes back over the hill before the last descent.

It was the kind of August heat that melts everything, and the shade of the woods on the way down was the best part of the ride.
