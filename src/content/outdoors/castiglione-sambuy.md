Twelve kilometres and 103 metres of climbing on foot, an almost flat walk across the plain to Sambuy and back, in February.

The way follows the bank of the Cimena canal and gravel lanes under bare oaks, between ploughed fields and green winter wheat, with the Alps white in the distance. Along the avenues the grass was still white with frost under rows of bare trees, and the hills stood hazy in the low winter sun.
