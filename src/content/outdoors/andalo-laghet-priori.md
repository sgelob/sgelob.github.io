Five kilometres and 71 metres of climbing on foot, a short loop round Andalo, in August.

The way follows gravel tracks along the edge of the forest and lanes beside the road, then climbs a little onto the meadows above the village, where Andalo spreads out below the wooded slopes of the Paganella. It comes back down on a gravel path and a wooden boardwalk across the grass.
