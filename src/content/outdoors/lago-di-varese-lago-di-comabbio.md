Fifty kilometres and 285 metres of climbing by bike, round the Lago di Varese and on to the Lago di Comabbio, at the end of July.

The cycle path runs all round the Lago di Varese, through meadows and woods along the shore, past wooden piers over the calm water and stretches of shore covered in lotus leaves, then crosses to the smaller Lago di Comabbio and comes back.

Off the western shore lies the Isolino Virginia, a tiny island with the oldest pile dwellings in the Alps, lived on from the early Neolithic to the end of the Bronze Age. In 2011 it became a UNESCO World Heritage Site, one of 111 prehistoric pile-dwelling sites around the Alps.
