Fifty-four kilometres and 908 metres of climbing on the cross-country bike, a big loop through the hills east of Turin in the heat of high summer: south over Baldissero to Andezeno and the lake of Arignano, east to Moncucco and Cinzano, and back through the two woods of Casalborgone and Castagneto, the Bosc Grand and the Bosco del Vaj, down to the Po.

The first part crosses the hill on trails through dry summer grass, with villages and castles on the hilltops all round and the stubble fields already cut below them. Past Andezeno the route reaches the lake of Arignano and follows the path along its dam, then climbs through the woods towards Moncucco, between tall maize and fields of sunflowers.

From Cinzano the way turns north into the woods of Casalborgone and Castagneto, shady tracks deep in last year’s leaves where the heat finally eased, with lookouts over wooded valleys rolling away to the mountains.

The way back drops to the plain by the Po and returns along straight gravel roads between green fields and sunflowers, the hill rising ahead.
