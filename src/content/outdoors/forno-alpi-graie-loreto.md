Five and a half kilometres and 497 metres of climbing on foot, from Forno Alpi Graie, at the head of the Val Grande di Lanzo, up to the sanctuary of Nostra Signora di Loreto and back, at the beginning of July.

The way follows the torrent between boulders below steep green slopes and rocky peaks, then climbs through the wood to the sanctuary, at about 1,340 metres, the last stretch up a flight of stone steps.

According to the Valli di Lanzo tourist board, the first chapel was built here in 1631 by a man from Forno, after an apparition of the Virgin was reported on the spot. The work was taken up again in 1843 and gave the sanctuary its present form.
