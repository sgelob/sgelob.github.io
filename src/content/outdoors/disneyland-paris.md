Sixteen and a half kilometres and 32 metres of climbing on foot in two days at Disneyland Paris: under six kilometres on the first afternoon and almost eleven on the second. Nobody goes to a theme park for a walk, but the tracker says it is one.

The map shows what a day in the park looks like: loops that start from the gates, go round the lands and come back to the central plaza again and again. The second day’s line is almost twice as long as the first.

It was a grey November, with the park already dressed for Christmas: a tall tree beside the bandstand at the top of the main street, garlands across it, and a parade of candy-coloured floats, with Santa on a golden sleigh above a crowd holding up phones and children.

In Adventureland a rock carved like a skull spills waterfalls through its teeth. Under the castle a dragon sleeps in a cave lit green and blue: at 27 metres from head to tail it was the largest animated figure ever built when the park opened in April 1992. In Frontierland the red rock spires of Big Thunder Mountain rise from the middle of a river. The Paris version is the only one that Disney built on an island: the trains leave the station on the bank and reach the mountain through a tunnel under the water. Next to it stands a row of yellow adobe houses with barred windows and wooden beams.

The second park, next door, got a new name in March 2026, Disney Adventure World, together with a lake and a land built around an ice mountain. After dark the mountain and its castle glow blue over the water, and the boulevard that leads there shines wet between the neon signs. In the first park the castle stands pink against the night clouds, above a crowd waiting in the dark.

The rest was done sitting down: a boat through a toy world where a red bus crosses a toy London and onion domes stand for Russia, a show on a stage of green vines and drums, and a steam train with red wheels pulling into the station above the gates. From inside the park a striped balloon on a cable shows above the autumn trees, hanging over the lake outside the gates.
