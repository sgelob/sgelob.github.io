Forty-three kilometres and 153 metres of climbing by bike, a loop across the plain of the Po, out to Chivasso and back by Brandizzo, Settimo and San Mauro, in May.

The way runs east along the river on gravel lanes between hedges and tracks across the meadows, under the bridges of the motorway, past a field red with poppies and ploughed fields below the wooded hills. Towards Chivasso it follows the cycle route along the Po that is meant to link Turin with Venice, then turns back west across the plain on canal banks and cycle paths beside the roads.

The last stretch follows the river again, past the weir and along gravel tracks between green fields, under the leafy oaks.
