Fifteen kilometres and 498 metres of climbing on foot, a loop over the hill by the Valle Garavaglia, Tetti Francesi, Cordova and Bardassano, in April.

Spring was at its height in the valley: the floor of the wood was white with wild garlic in flower, and the paths ran under walls of pale rock laced with roots. Along the banks the sandstone opens into small caves, and in places it is full of round pebbles, set in the rock like stones in a cake.

From Cordova the way drops towards Gassino and climbs again to Bardassano, through woods of fresh green leaves and meadows of tall grass, with a village standing on its hilltop beyond the trees. At Bardassano a cobbled lane ends at an old brick tower. On the way back a bright green meadow lay below the wooded hills.
