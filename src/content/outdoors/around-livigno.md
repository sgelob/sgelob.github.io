Eight kilometres and 192 metres of climbing on foot, along the slopes above Livigno, in August.

The path runs high along the side of the valley, among larches and pink fireweed in bloom, with the village spread out along the green valley floor below and big white clouds over the mountains.

Livigno is a duty-free zone. According to Focus, its privileges go back to 1538, when the community won its independence from Bormio over its pastures and woods, and a law of 1910 made the exemption official.
