Ten kilometres and 318 metres of climbing on the trail bike, a short loop on the hill between Castiglione and Gassino.

It climbs along the Strada della Trinità onto the slopes above Gassino, on a grassy track past young trees and wooden posts, then runs east towards Bussolino and comes back on path 82 and the woods below Bardassano.

Spring was just arriving: the trees on the open slopes already green, the woods still mostly bare, with the first leaves coming out over a single track covered in new growth.
