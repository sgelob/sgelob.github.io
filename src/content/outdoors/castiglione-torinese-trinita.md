Six kilometres and 151 metres of climbing on foot, a short loop on the hill between Castiglione and Gassino along the Strada della Trinità, in July.

The lanes run between hedges and meadows of tall grass, with greenhouses and orchards on the slopes and the wooded hills rolling away under big white clouds. From the top the plain spreads out towards the city, and a village stands on its hilltop beyond the trees.

The way down follows gravel roads under arching trees, past a trail sign and a small chapel at a fork in the road, through a green tunnel of leaves.
