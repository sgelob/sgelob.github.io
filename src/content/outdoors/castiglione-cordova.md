Twenty-three kilometres and 1,055 metres of climbing on the trail bike, without ever leaving the hill above Castiglione: up to Cordova, down into the Valle Garavaglia, and up again, over and over.

Cordova is the highest of Castiglione’s hamlets, at about 470 metres, a scatter of houses on the ridge with fewer than two hundred people living there. From it the slopes fall away steeply on both sides, and the trails make the most of it: short, sharp climbs on dirt and roots, and descents that are over almost as soon as they start.

Most of the riding is in the woods, which in May were at their greenest: a bright young wood near Cordova, a gravel track squeezed between tall grass and bushes by Tetti Francesi, an old track nearly lost under the weeds towards Tetti Varetto. Higher up, the ridge path along the Strada della Madonnina, marked in red and white, runs between oak trunks on a carpet of last year’s leaves with the light coming through from both sides.
