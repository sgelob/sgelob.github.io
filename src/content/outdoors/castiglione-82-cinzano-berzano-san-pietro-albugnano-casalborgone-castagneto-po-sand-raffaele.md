Fifty-five and a half kilometres and 1,502 metres of climbing on the cross-country bike, east into the Monferrato as far as Albugnano and Berzano, and back along the hills above the Po. The 82 in the title is the waymarked path 82, which takes the first part of the ride off the roads.

It starts in the woods towards Sciolze and Rivalba, where in May the grass and the young leaves had nearly swallowed the path, then climbs to Cinzano and on along the ridges to Albugnano, with the hills and fields of the Monferrato spread out below through the trees.

On the road from Albugnano towards Berzano stands the little church of San Pietro, now in Albugnano’s cemetery. It is first recorded in 1235, when the bishop of Vercelli gave it to the canons of Vezzolano, the abbey just below the village.

From Berzano the way drops to Casalborgone and climbs again over San Sebastiano, on white gravel tracks along the woods under big clouds. From the top the view opens over the wooded hills and the Po plain, with the basilica of Superga on its hill to the south-west. The last part runs through the trails of Sand Raffaele, on dry paths between hedges, and back.
