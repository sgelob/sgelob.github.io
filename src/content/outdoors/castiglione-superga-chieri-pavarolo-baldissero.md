Forty-three kilometres and 1,125 metres of climbing on the cross-country bike, up to Superga, down the far side of the hill to the edge of Chieri, and back through Andezeno, Montaldo, Pavarolo and Baldissero.

The first climb goes by Cordova to the top of the hill and the square in front of the basilica, quiet under a clear sky, with the trees along the terrace still green. From there the route follows the Via dei Colli and drops past Pino towards Chieri, on the roads of the Serra between the fields.

Then north-east through the farmland to Andezeno, on dirt trails along grassy banks under trees just starting to turn. Andezeno was one of the villages Chieri gathered in during the Middle Ages: in 1234 the counts of Biandrate, who held it, sold it to the Chieresi, and its Romanesque church of San Giorgio, now in the cemetery, goes back to the twelfth century.

The way back climbs through Montaldo and over the hill by Pavarolo and Baldissero.
