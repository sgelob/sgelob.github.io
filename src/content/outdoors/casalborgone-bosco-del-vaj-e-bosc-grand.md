Twelve kilometres and 403 metres of climbing on foot, a loop from Casalborgone up into the woods of the Bosco del Vaj and the Bosc Grand and back.

The way leaves the village on gravel lanes and grassy tracks between the meadows, then climbs into the woods, where the ground breaks through in pale banks of sandstone, eroded into odd shapes and laced with roots. The paths go up and down through tall thin woods, sunken tracks and muddy hollows, with the green hills showing through the trees and an old round water tank standing by a track.

Higher up a dirt road runs along the ridge above the wooded valleys, beside walls of sandstone, then a lane passes cypresses on the way back down. Casalborgone comes into view on its hill across the meadows, its church and castle on top. The castle was built to guard the road between Asti and Chivasso and later became the home of the Broglia counts; after years standing empty it has been restored. The last stretch climbs into the old upper village, the Leu, on cobbled lanes below the bell tower and past old brick houses covered in ivy.
