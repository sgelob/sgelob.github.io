Just under five kilometres and 138 metres of climbing on foot, a short loop from the hamlets of Castagneto Po up to the Bric del Vaj and back, at the beginning of July.

The way leaves the lane past an old farmhouse under the bamboo and goes into the wood on stony paths and faint trails through the undergrowth, under tall thin trees. Near the top a track runs deep between walls of soft rock and opens onto a viewpoint over the hills.

Down in the village stands the castle of Castagneto, now Villa Ceriana. The old fortress was destroyed in 1705; the present house was built in 1835 to designs by the court architect Ernesto Melano, and the park around it was laid out from 1847, according to the Turin tourist board. It takes its name from the banker and silk merchant who bought it in 1859.
