Thirteen kilometres and 856 metres of climbing, from Bousson above Cesana up to the Capanna Mautino and the Lago Nero, in July 2020.

Most of the climb is in the shade of the larches. The way starts beside a stream running over mossy boulders, then follows the long gravel road towards the lake, with narrow paths cutting its bends. Higher up the wood thins into clearings, small streams cross the track, and pale rock walls rise above the meadows.

Near the top the forest opens onto pastures with wooden huts, and the Lago Nero lies in a hollow among the larches, a house reflected in its still water. The Capanna Mautino, just above the lake at about 2,150 metres, belongs to the Sci Club Torino and serves walkers coming up from Bousson, about two hours below. The way back went down the same road, with the whole valley open below.
