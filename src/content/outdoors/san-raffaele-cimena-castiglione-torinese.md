Fourteen kilometres and 523 metres of climbing on foot, from San Raffaele Cimena up over the hill and across to Castiglione, in September under heavy clouds.

The way climbs out of the village on lanes past the old houses, and from the slopes the plain opens below: the towns spread out along the river, the hill of Superga rising beyond, and the clouds piling up over the mountains.

The route then follows small roads and lanes along the hillside, past farms and fields of tall grass, up a flight of steps between old houses and onto a terrace with a bench looking out over the valley. Between the roads it dives into the woods, down sunken paths and gullies with fallen trees across them and over slabs of bare rock.

The last part runs past a small chapel and along lanes between the hedges and back into the woods on paths under the trees.
