Five kilometres and 115 metres of climbing on the hill, a short walk down to the waterfall of the Rio Crivella, in March.

The fall was full, a white column of water dropping into the narrow gorge between walls of mossy conglomerate. Below it the stream runs over stones between steep banks, with fallen trunks across the gully.
