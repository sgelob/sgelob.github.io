Fifty-eight kilometres and 1,534 metres of climbing on the cross-country bike, a loop that crosses the Turin hill three times in the middle of autumn, under a low grey sky and on a carpet of leaves.

The way out follows the Po through San Mauro to the edge of the city, then climbs to Superga. From the road below the basilica Turin lay flat under the cloud, behind a screen of yellow leaves. A narrow trail buried in fallen leaves took me down the other side, and from the next valley the basilica was back on its ridge, small above the houses.

A second climb, up a gravel hairpin and along the road towards Pino, and a second descent, towards Baldissero. Here the tracks run between ploughed fields and vineyards gone yellow, and a flock of sheep was walking the track ahead of me. Then a muddy lane with puddles, under trees turned red.

Above Chieri, at Sant’Irene, a small round chapel painted yellow stands alone on a gravel clearing. From the town the last long climb goes back up to the ridge at Cento Croci and along the Via dei Colli, the road wet and edged with deep drifts of leaves, with the Alps just visible, white, at the end of the hills. At Monte Aman the basilica showed once more through the branches before the descent.
