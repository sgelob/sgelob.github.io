Thirty-three kilometres and 191 metres of climbing by bike, from Saint-Marcel along the Dora to Aosta and back, in July.

The cycle path follows the valley floor between hay meadows, with snowy peaks at the head of the valley, and comes into Aosta to the square in front of the arcaded town hall.

Aosta was founded by the Romans in 25 BC, after their victory over the Salassi, the people of the valley, and was named Augusta Praetoria after the emperor. The Arch of Augustus, built the same year to celebrate the victory, still stands at the entrance of the town, according to the regional tourist board.
