Eight kilometres and 173 metres of climbing on the hill above Castiglione, up to Tetti Francesi and back through the Valle Garavaglia past the Rio Crivella, in April.

The wood was at its best: fresh leaves on the trees and the whole floor white with wild garlic in flower along the path.
