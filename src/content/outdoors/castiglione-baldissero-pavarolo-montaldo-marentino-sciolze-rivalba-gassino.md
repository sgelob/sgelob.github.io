Fifty-two kilometres and 1,341 metres of climbing on the cross-country bike, a long loop round the hills east of Turin: south to Baldissero and Pavarolo, out through Montaldo to Marentino, and back north by Sciolze, Rivalba and Gassino.

Autumn was at its height under a low grey sky. The lanes were strewn with yellow leaves, and the trails over the hill towards Baldissero were half buried under brambles and fallen leaves.

Past Pavarolo and Montaldo the country opens out into green fields and rolling hills, gravel tracks along the fences with Marentino on its hilltop ahead, and the yellow church of Avuglione, one of Marentino’s hamlets, standing over its cobbled square.

The way back turns north through Montaldo and towards Sciolze, where the castle of Bardassano comes into view on its wooded hill with the basilica of Superga faint on the horizon behind it, and on through Rivalba, past a small chapel at a road junction, before the last descent towards Gassino.
