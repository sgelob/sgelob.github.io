Eleven kilometres and 662 metres of climbing on foot, from Dondena up to the Lago Misérin and back, at the beginning of August.

The way climbs from Dondena on paths across the green pastures, past horses grazing beside the track below the cliffs. On a rock face above the path a whole herd of ibex was clinging to the ledges. Higher up the paths wind across the high pastures to the lake, dark below its rocky peaks, and the way back follows the stony track down past yellow flowers, above the deep valley.
