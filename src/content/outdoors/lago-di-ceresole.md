Twelve kilometres and 97 metres of climbing on foot, a loop round the Lago di Ceresole, in the middle of June.

The way follows the shore from Pian della Balma round to the village and back along the far side, over the top of the dam. The water was a deep turquoise, but low: wide bare shores of pale rock and sand ran all round it below the forested mountains.

The lake is a reservoir. According to Wikipedia, it was made between 1925 and 1931, when Turin’s electricity authority dammed the Orco for a hydroelectric plant, and the water covered dozens of scattered houses in the basin.
