Sixty-four kilometres and 1,300 metres of climbing on the cross-country bike, east out of the Turin hill into the Monferrato as far as Cocconato, and back along the Po.

The first part follows farm tracks and paths in the woods behind Gassino and Rivalba, some of them so overgrown at the end of summer that the brambles closed over the way, then climbs past Casalborgone and Cinzano onto the ridges on the Asti side, with the hills folding away in every direction and the Alps faint on the horizon.

Below Albugnano, the hilltop village known as the balcony of the Monferrato, stands the abbey of Santa Maria di Vezzolano. Legend credits Charlemagne with founding it; the first document that names it dates from 1095, and the church keeps a rare stone screen across its nave, carved and painted in the early thirteenth century.

Further east, past Marmorito, the route climbs to Cocconato, a village of brick houses round its church tower, called the Riviera del Monferrato for a climate mild enough for palms and olive trees. It was the seat of the Radicati counts until it passed to Savoy in 1586, and from the terraces at the top the plain lies open below.

Then the long way down to the river, through San Sebastiano and Castagneto, on flat roads between the fields.
