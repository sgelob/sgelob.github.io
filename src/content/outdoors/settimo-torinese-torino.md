Nearly fifteen kilometres and 39 metres of climbing, a Sunday ride in June from Settimo along the Po to the edge of Turin and back.

The way runs out through San Mauro and down to the river, then follows the gravel paths along the bank to the Meisino, where the Stura di Lanzo flows into the Po. The wetlands around the confluence are a nature reserve of about 45 hectares, among the best places in the city for birds; on the Isolone Bertolla, the island in the river, grey herons have nested since 1986.

Beyond the Meisino the path keeps to the water, between the green bank and the big trees, past a rust-red footbridge under a sky of summer clouds, before turning back the same way to San Mauro and Settimo.
