Ten kilometres and 282 metres of climbing on foot, a loop over the hills towards Cordova, Tetti None and Tetti Varetto, in April.

Spring had just come to the hills: the meadows were green and full of dandelions, the trees were coming into leaf, and below the slopes the villages and the plain stretched away under a clear sky. The way follows lanes and paths along the ridge between the hamlets.
