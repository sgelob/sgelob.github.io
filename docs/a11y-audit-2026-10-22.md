## Accessibility Audit: olegs.be (Astro rebuild)
**Standard:** WCAG 2.1 AA | **Date:** 2026-10-22

Scope: 15 pages of the `astro preview` build at http://localhost:4321 (`/`, `/photos/`, `/photos/genre/abandoned-places/`, `/photos/country/usa/`, `/gallery/venezia-italy/`, `/gallery/kiev4a-type2-soviet-35mm-rangefinder-film-camera/`, `/blog/`, `/2025/04/podcast-naval-ravikant-dure-verita-felicita-natura-umana/`, `/2017/04/video-slide-wordpress-wordcamp-torino-2017/`, `/about/`, `/events/`, `/books/`, `/quotes/`, `/quote-author/aristotle/`, `/this-does-not-exist/`).

Method: axe-core 4.10.2 injected into each page in the gstack headless Chromium (tags `wcag2a, wcag2aa, wcag21a, wcag21aa, best-practice`); manual review of the source and the rendered DOM (landmarks, headings, `lang`, accessible names, tab order, computed focus styles); target sizes at 375×812; reflow at 320 px and 640 px plus a WCAG 1.4.12 text-spacing override; contrast computed with the WCAG relative-luminance formula. Hero contrast was computed against the **real cover originals** (`~/olegs-be-data/uploads`, all 85 covers), resized like `object-fit: cover`, with the `GalleryHero` scrims composited per pixel row; the figure reported is the 95th-percentile brightest pixel inside each text box. The PhotoSwipe lightbox was judged from `Lightbox.astro` and PhotoSwipe 5.4.4's source, because `img.olegs.be` does not resolve and the images do not load.

### Summary
**Issues found:** 17 | **Critical:** 0 | **Major:** 4 | **Minor:** 13

axe reports only one violation across the 15 pages: `frame-title` on the WordCamp post. The site has a sound base: real landmarks, one `h1` per page, a visible `:focus-visible` ring, `aria-current` on the navigation, no keyboard traps, and no reflow at 320 px. Most remaining barriers are in three places: text over the cover photos, photo alt text, and language markup in a bilingual site.

### Priority Fixes
1. **Hero contrast (P1):** use px-based scrims and a fixed nav band, and move the caption below the cover on mobile. This fixes 59/85 covers on mobile nav alone.
2. **Iframe titles (R1):** add `title` to the 75 embeds and enforce it with a rehype check at build time.
3. **Language of parts (U1):** add `lang={post.data.lang}` on home post items and a `lang` field for the 11 Italian quotes.
4. **Photo alt text (P2):** add an optional `alt` to the photo schema, shorten the fallback to "Photo N of M", and backfill gallery by gallery.
5. **Card alt duplication (P3):** set `alt=""` on `GalleryCard` images.
6. **Arrow glyphs and pager labels (O2):** wrap the glyphs in `aria-hidden` and add "Older/Newer" text.
7. **Skip link and a stronger two-tone focus ring (O1, O4).**
8. **Language of the chrome on Italian pages (U2), and `/blog/` `lang`.**
9. **Lightbox dialog name and live counter (R2).**
10. **Polish:** heading levels (P4, P5), caption wrap (P7), link underlines (P8), duplicate title (O5), touch padding (O3), hero alt for the 4 covers not in the grid (P6).
